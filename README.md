# 🇮🇳 SchemeMatch AI

## AI-Powered Government Scheme Discovery & Eligibility Assistant

SchemeMatch AI is a web application designed to help citizens discover government welfare schemes based on their needs and understand scheme benefits and eligibility in a simple way.

## 🎯 Problem

Many citizens are unaware of government schemes that they may be eligible for. Information is often scattered across different websites and can be difficult to understand.

## 💡 Our Solution

SchemeMatch AI provides a simple platform where users can:

- 🔍 Discover relevant government schemes
- 📋 View scheme details and benefits
- ✅ Understand eligibility requirements
- 🤖 Get AI-assisted guidance
- 🌐 Access information in multiple languages
- 💾 Save useful schemes
- 📱 Use a simple and responsive interface
